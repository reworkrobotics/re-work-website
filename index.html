Why us

As a team of passionate students, we are fully prepared to build a new robotics program from the ground up. We create to innovate, review to learn, and rework to advance. Our process of perfection is endless improvement,and that's what drives us forward

A Foundation for Success

A rookie team's greatest asset is its support system.

We are proud to have established a mentorship partnership with the robotics program at Dr. Norman Bethune C.I., home of some of Ontario's most successful FTC teams.

Mr. Vincent Lu, Assistant Curriculum Leader for Tech and Staff Supervisor for Bethune's five robotics teams, has personally committed to guiding our team.

Your pledge supports a rookie team that is already backed by an established and successful organization.

  • Expert guidance for our teacher-advisor.
  • Student-to-student mentorship from a veteran FTC team.
  • A proven roadmap for building a successful program.
